DATA SOURCES REFERENCE FILE
============================
IDP Project — Urban Infrastructure Prediction System
All numbers used in infrastructure.csv — where each came from


1. COORDINATES (Latitude & Longitude)
---------------------------------------
Source: OpenStreetMap Nominatim API (https://nominatim.openstreetmap.org)
Method: Auto-fetched via Python script (data/collect_data.py)
Date collected: July 2025
Trust level: 5/5 — OpenStreetMap is maintained by millions of contributors worldwide
All 15 cities verified: Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad,
Ahmedabad, Pune, Surat, Jaipur, Lucknow, Kanpur, Nagpur, Indore, Visakhapatnam


2. POPULATION (1980-2025)
--------------------------
Source: UN World Urbanization Prospects (https://population.un.org/wup/)
Dataset: Urban agglomeration populations, 5-year intervals
Method: Downloaded from UN city datasets and formatted into population.csv
Trust level: 5/5 — this is the international gold standard used by World Bank, IMF, and all governments
Years covered: 1980, 1985, 1990, 1995, 2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, 2025
