Data Sources

Every number in IDP is traceable. Here are the primary datasets, standards, and references behind the predictions.

👥 Population Data

Census of India

Primary source. Decadal census data (1981, 1991, 2001, 2011, 2021) for urban agglomerations. Conducted by Office of the Registrar General & Census Commissioner.

Primary
World Bank Open Data

Urban population and growth rate datasets used for cross-validation of Census figures. API: data.worldbank.org

Secondary
UN World Urbanization Prospects 2024

Used to verify long-term urban growth trajectory for major metropolitan regions.

Reference

🏗️ Infrastructure Data

Ministry of Education — UDISE+ 2021-22

School counts per city. Unified District Information System for Education Plus tracks all government and private schools.

National Health Mission — Health Facility Registry

Hospital and clinic counts per district, aggregated to city level. Includes public and empanelled private facilities.

Ministry of Urban Development — City Transport Reports

Public bus fleet sizes for major cities from annual transport reports and NUTP (National Urban Transport Policy) surveys.

Ministry of Road Transport & Highways

Road network length (urban) from the annual report on road statistics in India.

📐 Planning Norms & Standards

Metric Norm Used Standard
🏫 Schools 1 per 5,000 people UDPFI Guidelines (MoUD)
🏥 Hospitals 1 per 25,000 people National Health Policy 2017
🚌 Buses 1 per 1,200 people NUTP / MoUD Transport Norms
🛣️ Roads 1 km per 800 people IRC:SP:20 Rural Roads Manual
