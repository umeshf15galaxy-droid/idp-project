What IDP Does

Every Layer of the Urban Future

From raw census numbers to actionable infrastructure planning — one system.

🤖

Multi-Model AI

Compares Linear, Polynomial, and Exponential regression models. Selects the best by cross-validated R² score automatically.

📊

Real Census Data

Trained on 5 decades of Indian census data (1981–2021) for 15 major cities. Predictions are grounded in reality.

🏗️

Infrastructure Analysis

Translates population into required schools, hospitals, buses, and roads using official Indian urban planning norms.

⚠️

Shortage Warnings

Auto-generates critical and warning alerts when projected deficits exceed 10% or 30% of requirement.

📈

Visual Charts

Interactive population growth timeline with confidence bands, plus infrastructure comparison charts.

🎯

Confidence Intervals

Every prediction includes uncertainty ranges — ±10% for 15-year outlook, ±20% for long-term horizons.

How It Works

Three Steps. One Insight.

1

Select Your City & Year

Choose from 15 major Indian cities. Set any target year from 2025 to 2100. Optionally override current infrastructure counts.

2

AI Runs 3 Models Simultaneously

Linear, Polynomial, and Exponential regression compete. The one with the highest cross-validated R² score wins and makes the final prediction.

3

Get Your Full Infrastructure Blueprint

See predicted population, required schools/hospitals/buses/roads, current deficits, and visual charts — all in one dashboard.
